# FAQ

## About the course

### What is the course goal?

To discover biomarkers and evaluate the findings for correctness,
from a **multi-omics dataset**
using **computational approaches**,
starting from a research question.

- [ ] Discuss with Prasoon if dataset is multi-omics

Course non-goals:

- Describe best practices on the wetlab parts
- List all the computational techniques to discover biomarkers
- Use single-omics datasets

- [ ] Discuss with Prasoon if dataset is not single-omics

### Does the course teach how to verify and validate a biomarker?

Yes.

- [ ] Discuss with Prasoon

Even though the course is called 'Biomarker Discovery' and not
'Biomarker discovery, verification and validation'
(the three phases mentioned in `[Nakayasu et al., 2021]`)

???- question "What are these three phases?"

    These are three phases in biomarker discovery mentioned
    in `[Nakayasu et al., 2021]`:

    Phase|Name
    -----|------------
    1    |Discovery
    2    |Verification
    3    |Validation

## Teaching

### How is the course designed?

The course starts from a research question
and follows the researcher into doing
biomarker discovery.

This is different than the more classical approach:
to enumerate all biomarker discovery methods.

### How is the course taught?

We use the best practices in teaching, according to the academic literature.

You will see:

- Active teaching
- Interaction

You will not see:

- Lectures longer than 10 minutes: we will need to explain why things are
  important

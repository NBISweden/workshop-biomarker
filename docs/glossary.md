# Glossary

An overview of definitions and abbreviations.

## Biomarker

'A biomarker is a measurable characteristic that reflects the
physiological or pathological state of an organism' `[Ng et al., 2023]`

## Diagnostic biomarker 

'A diagnostic biomarker detects or confirms the presence of a
disease or condition of interest, or identifies an individual
with a subtype of the disease'
(inaccessible reference 3 from `[Califf, 2018]`).

## Endotype

An endotype is one of many biological features and/or pathways
to achieve the same phenotype.

'Although patients may clinically manifest in a similar manner,
an **endotype** defines a subgroup of patients who share a
common underlying biology or pathway mechanism, which
drives the manifestation of the disease or phenotype' `[Ng et al., 2023]`

## Prognostic biomarkers

'prognostic' comes from 'prognosticum' (second 'o' has a horizontal line
above it),
which means 'signs predicting what sort of weather is coming'.
In the context of biomarkers, these used to determine which stage of a disease
a patient is, to give stage-specific treatment.

For example, type 1 diabetes mellitus is diagnosed as such from
blood glucose concentration, yet there is an earlier stage
of immunological changes.

## PSP

Short for 'Progressive Supranuclear Palsy',
a brain disease.
